Records are written when something changes - an entry discovered or pruned, a certificate published, withdrawn or moved between states, a point of contact or address edited, a project joined or left, an alert delivered or refused. A sweep that finds the directory exactly as it left it writes nothing here. Records outlive what they describe: an entry pruned from the directory leaves its history behind, named by the DN it had.
